# E-U256-003 — typed UInt256 bitwise operations and logical shifts

## Goal

Extend the reusable EVM wide-word component instead of teaching the main emitter six new source
recipes or asking contracts to manipulate four `UInt64` limbs themselves.

## Delivered contract

- `Evm.WideWord.Bitwise` names AND, OR, and XOR; `Evm.WideWord.Shift` names logical left and
  right shift. There are no numeric operation tags in the new API.
- `WideWord.Query` carries fixed-shape binary, complement, and shift plans through the existing
  `Component.wideWord` bridge. The target interpreter packs each `UInt256` once, performs one Yul
  word operation, caches that result, and projects the four fixed limbs.
- `Evm.Sdk.UInt256` exposes `bitAnd`, `bitOr`, `bitXor`, `complement`, `shiftLeft`, and
  `shiftRight`. Shift amounts are bounded source `UInt64` values; EVM semantics produce zero for
  amounts greater than or equal to 256.
- `Examples.Evm.Wide` is only the contract fixture. The reusable semantics and physical interpreter
  remain in the target-owned SDK/component modules.

This slice does not change default `UInt64` operators, introduce signed words, or decide the
divide-by-zero policy needed by the later division/modulo slice.

## Fail-closed and resource boundary

- Binary operations require exactly eight limbs, complement four limbs, and shifts four limbs plus
  one scalar amount. Limb selectors outside 0..3 and wrong arities are rejected before emission.
- The implementation has no runtime buffer, heap allocation, storage handle, CALL, LOG, or hidden
  state effect. Intermediates are bounded Yul stack words owned by the sole `WideWord` interpreter.

## Evidence

- Focused Lean build: 132 jobs (`Tests.WideSpec` plus the full EVM build-spec registry gate).
- All 20 registered EVM contracts compile with solc 0.8.34.
- Anvil 20/20 passes, including cross-limb bitwise operations, 65-bit shifts, 256-bit truncation,
  and the EVM `shift ≥ 256 → 0` rule.
- The other 19 contracts retain byte-identical Yul/bytecode/ABI artifacts. Wide changes to digest
  `faa49d815b13ff57`, emits 19,247 bytes of Yul, and has 2,568-byte deployment bytecode.
- Ownership and whitespace gates pass.
