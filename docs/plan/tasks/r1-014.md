# R1-014 — Bounded Queue / BitSet semantic contracts

## Goal

Complete the shared finite-collection vocabulary with reusable FIFO and packed-bit semantics,
without turning either structure into a new opcode, target wire format, persistent pointer, or
heap-backed container.

## Delivered

- `Core.Collections.BoundedQueue α capacity` is a fixed-frame ring with explicit `UInt32` head and
  active length. It provides bounded logical reads, peek, checked push/pop, clear, full/empty/size,
  wrap-around, and canonical empty-state validation.
- `Core.Collections.BoundedBitSet capacity` packs a type-level number of bits into a fixed Vector of
  `UInt64` words. It provides checked insert/remove/update, membership, clear, and exact word-count
  calculation; out-of-range indexes cannot alias through modulo arithmetic.
- Inactive queue slots may retain stale payloads but are unreachable. Neither contract performs
  allocation, stores an address, or selects capacity at runtime.

## Ownership and target boundary

These are target-neutral logical laws only. This slice does not mark the operations `pf_inline`,
does not add Core or target Ops/IR/Emit cases, and does not claim account/storage persistence.
SVM may bind Queue/BitSet to fixed account bytes and indexes; EVM may bind them to fixed slots or a
bounded bitmap namespace. Those bindings require separate resource/layout qualification and must
not invent a shared physical layout.

## Verification

`Tests.CoreCollectionsSpec.QueueAndBitSetSemantics` covers FIFO capacity, logical indexing,
wrap-around, canonical drain, malformed-frame rejection, packed word boundaries, mutation, clear,
and out-of-range rejection. The focused and complete Lean builds plus ownership/no-sorry/whitespace
gates pass.
